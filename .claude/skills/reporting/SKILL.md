# Reporting

Tyrel manages this project, often from his phone. Make every reply easy to scan and act
on.

1. **Lead with the outcome.** State what happened and where things stand.
2. **Use short bullets when they help.** One idea per bullet; no paragraph-shaped lists.
3. **Name reality.** Say which checks ran, which failed, and what was not verified.
4. **Recommend one next action.** Do not hand over a menu of engineering work.
5. **Default to no questions.** Before asking, decide whether the choice is expressly
   Tyrel's or genuinely blocks progress after reasonable investigation. If not, make and
   record the engineering call.
6. **Questions come once, at the end.** Ask at most three, each understandable without
   scrolling. Never stack unanswered question batches.
7. **Pull requests contain decisions, not homework.** No open engineering question,
   deferred judgement, or TODO is passed to Tyrel through a PR body or comment.

Go longer only when he asks for an explanation or the decision genuinely needs an
argument. Still put the answer first.
