Follow-up to brief 0022, same worktree (work/pk-skew), same rules (clean-room rule, allowed paths, checks, commit trailers, stop conditions all unchanged). An independent build-side reviewer re-checked 64c8fe0. All four losses B1-B4 are fixed and the noise, disagreement and dpi items are done. But the fixes treat texture as ink, which sends ordinary pages to review and makes one confident wrong box. 11c1cb1 handled every case below correctly, so these are regressions. Keep B1-B4 fixed. For each, add a failing test first, then fix. Report the head and what each test proved.

R1. Mild paper mottling sends the page to review and spreads the content box over the sheet (content.py ink_map and the faint_contrast = 20 setting). A 300 dpi text page with mottling of SD 4-5 grey levels in 1-2 mm patches gives "58 faint marks kept" at confidence 0.5, and the box grows from [354,360,1446,2160] to [39,63,1863,2556]; at SD 6.5 it is the whole sheet. A blank mottled page is called not blank; foxing spots 25-40 levels deep do the same. Cause: flattening puts the local brightest paper at 255, so ordinary paper already sits about 2 SD below it. Fix: measure contrast from the local paper median, not the local maximum; set the faint level from the page's own measured paper noise (for example max(20, k times a robust SD)); let a faint component widen the box only if it is stroke-shaped (thin and elongated), and count and flag the others without widening. Tests: a mottled text page at SD 5 gives no flag and the same box as the clean page; a blank mottled page is blank; a foxed page keeps its box.
R2. The strip check widens the page box to the frame on common backdrops, with a flag (pagebox.py _keep_ink_in_strips). A dark backdrop with grain SD about 8 moves the left edge out 177 px; at SD 16 all four sides go to the frame. A grey-scale target or ruler on a dark backdrop pulls that side out to include it. Archive scans usually carry one. Fix: treat strip ink as writing only when it is joined to the paper or within a few millimetres of the paper edge; skip regions where the grid or ruler finders find a target; ignore texture whose run density matches the rest of the backdrop. Tests: textured dark backdrop, SD 8 and 16; a target and a ruler on a dark backdrop. Each gives the paper's box with no flag. Keep the gutter-shadow-over-writing case flagged.
R3. On a pale backdrop with a ruler or target, the page box takes in the target at confidence 0.9 and no flag, and the evidence wrongly says "kept at the frame edge (no backdrop there)" (pagebox.py _pale_step). The ruler breaks the uniform-margin test, so that side is never walked; the content box then takes in the ruler too. Fix: let the pale-margin test tolerate a minority of non-uniform lines (for example use the median of the first few lines along the side); when a side is not walked but the frame edge is much paler than the paper, flag it rather than saying there is no backdrop. Tests: pale backdrop with a ruler, and with a target.
