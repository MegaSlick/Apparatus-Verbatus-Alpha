O1. Confident wrong half turns on small printed type. Upright serif 8 pt at 150 dpi (5 of 5 seeds), sans 7 pt at 300 dpi (5 of 5) and a printed sans 9 pt spread at 300 dpi (3 of 3) give 2 turns at confidence 0.55 to 0.74 with no flag, in every turn; the old code was right or flagged. Concrete case: the reviewer's `gen.case_print(150, 8, "serif", seed=158)` returns 2, confidence 0.56, no flags, for an upright page. Cause: the up-down vote depends on core_share (thresholds_split.toml around line 70, 0.6; orient.py around 187). On these pages the score falls steadily as core_share rises (+16.2 at 0.4, -3.1 at 0.6, -18.4 at 0.8), while pagekit's own synthetic cursive rises, so 0.6 was fitted to one kind of page. Mean over standard error (orient.py around 255) measures how consistently the strips agree, not whether they are right, so a steady bias from a misplaced core band reads as confidence. Failures occur at an x-height of about 6 to 7 px in the working copy, which a 300 dpi spread reduced to 1600 px reaches. Fix the cause, not the setting: find each line's core band from that line's own profile (for example its densest run of rows) rather than one share for the strip; and add a guard so that a vote whose sign changes across a range of reasonable core estimates is uncertain and flagged. Lowering core_share to 0.4 alone removed every wrong answer on the reviewer's set, but do not rely on one value. Also make the score antisymmetric under a half turn (score both orientations and take half the difference): today it is -3.12 at turn 0 against +2.48 at turn 2 on the same page, because the strip grid starts at x=0 and drops leftover columns (orient.py around 177) and the first and last lines are handled unevenly. Tests: small-type printed pages and a printed spread at turns 0 and 2, serif and sans, 150 and 300 dpi, several seeds; all right or flagged, never wrong without a flag.
O2. A real flourish touching the backdrop band loses its overhang flag (split.py around 594-614). Marks within touch_px of a dark band are removed from `straddling` entirely, so a pen stroke crossing the fold that touches the top or bottom backdrop band is no longer flagged, though spec 0003 requires it. Concrete case: a spread at 300 dpi with a fold at x=1000, a dark band over rows 0 to 59, and a 5 px stroke from (880,120) through (1000,60) to (1160,115), touching the band: the old code flagged "overhangs it by 10.2 mm"; now no flag, and the evidence says the mark is "joined to a dark band... not counted as writing". The same at 3 px from the band and at the bottom band. Fix: excuse only marks that are thick like backdrop (for example ones that survive an erosion wider than a pen stroke), and keep pen strokes in the overhang check; keep your backdrop-wedge test passing.

Smaller: very large hands (8 mm x-height, about 6 lines), handwritten ledgers and pages with notes turned 90 degrees are now flagged where the old code was right; that is safe, but see whether the per-line core band recovers them.
