# Finding: a grey profile chosen for a whole monochrome collection

Reader brief sha256: bd3e1c50d4f2819416114fdebe2da7d245b3d9485f5f30c6a1589476880d56c2

## Situation

A person knows a whole collection is monochrome microfilm and wants to apply a grey preparation to a named batch at once rather than decide page by page. Later files, or files from another collection, may not share that property.

## Pagekit's observed behaviour

Already: settings apply to a whole run, and a person can set values per page.

Differently: there is no colour choice to apply, since the colour mode always follows the source.

Not yet: a named grey profile applied to a stated batch under a stated review policy; a record of which pages it covered; a check that still raises colour evidence on a page inside the batch.

## General technique

A batch rule is a recorded decision with an explicit scope: which captures it applies to, who set it, when, and what review policy goes with it. It does not extend to captures added later, and it does not silence a page-level check: a page in the batch that shows real colour is still raised for a decision. This is the ordinary practice of scoping an approval to a named set of items.
Source: general knowledge

## Settings in general terms

- The scope: an explicit list or identity of the covered captures, fixed when the rule is set.
- The review policy: chosen by the person; should state whether pages with colour evidence are held, kept in source mode or sent to review.
