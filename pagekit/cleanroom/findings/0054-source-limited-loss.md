# Finding: loss already in the source is recorded apart from loss made by preparation

## Situation

Writing may already be cut off by the frame edge, hidden under a finger or a fold, or covered by another sheet when the capture was made. That is not the preparation's fault and cannot be fixed by it, but it must not be confused with loss the preparation causes, and the missing part must not be invented.

## Pagekit's observed behaviour

Already: writing cut off by the frame edge on one side is flagged as a possible neighbour strip; areas outside the source are filled with the measured paper colour, never with invented content; the crop check measures ink discarded by a crop.

Differently: writing cut by the frame edge is read as a sign of a neighbour strip, not recorded as a source limitation.

Not yet: a source-limitation record (clipped at capture, occluded) kept separate from loss counted against the preparation; an operator note on a source condition.

## General technique

Account for evidence in two ledgers: what the capture holds, and what the prepared pages keep of it. Loss is measured only against the second. Marks touching the frame edge are noted as possibly clipped at capture. Edge filling stays neutral (paper colour) and is marked as synthetic; reflection, repetition, synthesis or inpainting are not used, since they create marks that were never there.
