# Goals — Apparatus Verbatus

What this project is for. Every stage, plan, and decision is measured against these.

These are the direction of travel. Goals 1 and 2 are not verifiable and are not meant to
be — they are what we aim at. A *claim* that we have achieved something is a different
thing, and Governance 10 binds it.

## 1. Capture every act

Near-100% text capture. **A missed act is worse than a poorly read act.** A poor reading
can be corrected later; an act nobody knows exists is lost silently and forever.

## 2. Read every act accurately

Near-100% transcription accuracy against the ink itself — not against what a witness
reported, and not against what a plausible act of that period would say.

## 3. The Perlector reads

Every act is actively read, with page context available. Every page is accounted for:
marked out by the Designator and checked for completeness by the Recensor, so nothing on
it goes unexamined.

Witness readings are clues that sharpen the Perlector's own reading. They are never
options to choose between, and a witness reading is never itself an output.

## 4. Export completely

Everything read leaves the pipeline: the established text, its provenance, and its link
back to the exact image region. In whatever formats are asked for.

What happens to the export afterwards — search, research, correction — is not this
project.

## 5. Stay traceable

Every result returns to the exact region of ink it came from: the page, the crop, the
witnesses that saw it, and the reading that was produced.
