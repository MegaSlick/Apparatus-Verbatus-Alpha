# Governance — Apparatus Verbatus

How we are allowed to pursue the goals. These bind every stage, every plan, and every
working session. No dates, defects, or audits — those live in the workbench.

## Who decides

**Tyrel.** He is the only human in these rules.

Only Tyrel changes this file. Only Tyrel grants the permissions it requires, declares
the pipeline proven, or approves an exclusion. **No automated agent may act as the human
in any rule here**, and no session may amend these documents and call it deliberate.

## Scope

The pipeline takes source images in and puts established readings out. Import to export.

Training, research, search, and correction happen elsewhere and are not governed here.

---

## 0. When a rule and a goal pull apart, stop

These rules exist to serve the goals. Where following one to the letter would cost an act,
lose a reading, or hide a result, that is not a decision to resolve in the moment — say so
and hand it to Tyrel. A rule obeyed into a lost act has failed at the thing it was for.

## 1. Quality over speed

Extra passes, extra effort, and slower runs are acceptable costs. Speed is never a
reason to read less carefully.

## 2. Nothing is lost silently

An act may be uncertain, unreadable, or held for review. It may not disappear behind a
successful status. A partial result is visibly partial; "complete" is refused unless
everything reconciles.

This covers what we produce *about* the pipeline as well as what it produces: a finding, a
review, a defect, a decision. A result that exists only in a transcript nobody reads has
been lost, whatever produced it.

## 3. The Perlector never picks

It reads the ink. Witness testimony informs the reading; it never substitutes for it.
No stage selects a winner among witnesses.

Where the Perlector reconciles its own readings, it does so **against the image**, and
that reconciliation is bound by this rule exactly as any other step would be. A picker
rebuilt under another name is still a picker.

## 4. Evidence is never overwritten

The Exemplar is immutable. Testimony and readings are layers — each records, none
replaces what came before. Testimony is always retained.

## 5. One text, everywhere

One established text per act, projected identically into every format the pipeline
produces. No deliverable may carry a different reading than another.

Showing witness testimony *as testimony* is not a different reading.

## 6. Provenance travels with the record

Every stored reading carries the resolved identity and revision of the model that
produced it, at the moment it was produced. Configuration protects reproducibility
going forward; the record itself protects the past.

## 7. The pipeline does not gate model behaviour

Accuracy, honesty, fabrication, parroting — these are not corrected here. The pipeline's
obligation toward the model is to **feed it completely and measure it honestly**.

## 8. No live pod without permission

A live pod requires Tyrel's explicit permission for that session. Shutdown is verified
against provider state and billing, never inferred from an acknowledgement.

## 9. Prove before scale

A small, reasonable test must perform well before anything scales. Tyrel decides what
counts as small, reasonable, and well.

## 10. Measure honestly

A metric that cannot be measured is a failure, not a pass. Claims are made only about
what was actually measured.

**The instrument may not constrain what it measures.** A grading prompt that sets a severity
floor, budgets a confidence level, or tells a reader which way to argue reports the
instruction rather than the finding. Ask for everything and filter afterwards.

Goals are what we aim at. Claims are what we assert. Do not confuse them.

## 11. Recovery is bounded, and it recovers coverage

A stage that can request rework may do so a fixed, recorded number of times before
handing to Tyrel. Nothing disappears inside a recovery loop.

Recovery exists for **completeness and coverage** — a missed region, a cut crop, a
continuation. Content quality is not its business: suspected fabrication or a bad
reading may be **flagged for review**, never re-rolled until it looks better.
